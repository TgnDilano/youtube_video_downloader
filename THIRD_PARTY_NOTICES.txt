THIRD PARTY NOTICES
====================

TubeXMate bundles third-party command-line tools that are executed as
separate processes. They are downloaded at build time into the app bundle.

yt-dlp
------
Source: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
License: Unlicense / Public Domain
"A command-line program to download videos from YouTube and other sites."

ffmpeg / ffprobe
-----------------
Source: https://ffmpeg.org
The bundled ffmpeg and ffprobe executables are compiled from the FFmpeg
source code.
- macOS build (evermeet.cx): licensed under LGPL v2.1 or later.
- Windows build (gyan.dev): licensed under GPL v3 (includes components under
  the GPL). 
This application invokes ffmpeg as a standalone process and does not link
against it. License texts:
- LGPL v2.1: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
- GPL v3: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html
- FFmpeg source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#releases

These tools may be updated in-app via the Settings > Engine "Update" action.
